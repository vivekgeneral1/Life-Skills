What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

Answer: In active thinking mode, you are focused on the topic. In diffused thinking mode you are relaxed and thinking thinking lightly.

### Question 4
According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

Answer: 
1. Break down the complex skill into smallest individual skills and start learning them individually.
2. Learn just enough to be able to correct yourself and start practice as soon as possibele.
3. Remove distractions that get in the way of your practice.
4. Actually practice the skill for atleast 20 hours.
