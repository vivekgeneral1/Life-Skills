# Listening and Active Communication

### Question 1
What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)

Answer:
+ Don't get distracted by your own thoughts.
+ Focus on the topic.
+ Don't interrupt the speaker.
+ Only after the speaker has finished can you respond.
+ Use door-openers to convey to the speaker that you are listening attentively.
+ Your body language should reflect your attentiveness.
+ You can note down important points.
+ Paraphrase to make sure everybody is on the same page.

  

### Question 2
According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)

Answer:
+ Listen more, talk less
+ If the topic is personal, don't deviate.
+ Repeat what the speaker has said.
+ Try to understand the feelings.
+ Try to understand the speaker's point of view.
+ Show empathy.


### Question 3
What are the obstacles in your listening process?

Answer:
+ Tension
+ Boredom
+ Tiredness
+ Lazyness
+ Distractions


### Question 4
What can you do to improve your listening?

Answer
+ Focus on the topic/speaker.
+ Sleep well at night.
+ Maintain eye contact.
+ Have a good body posture.
+ Keep distractions away.


### Question 5
When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

Answer: I switch to a Passive communication style when I am talking to someone in formal settings, e.g. talking to a manager or boss.


### Question 6
When do you switch into Aggressive communication style in your day to day life?

Answer: I switch to an Aggressive communication style when I am annoyed with my friends and family.

### Question 7
When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

Answer: I switch to a Passive Aggressive communication style when I am with my friends in a fun environment.
