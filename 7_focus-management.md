# Focus Management

### Question 1
What is Deep Work?
### Answer:
Working on a task while and giving it your total focus is Deep Work.

---
### Question 2
According to the author, how to do deep work properly, in a few points?
### Answer:
1. Schedule time for Distractions.
2. Have a Deep Work Ritual.
3. Plan tomorrow's work in Today's evening. 

---
### Question 3
How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?
### Answer:
+ Play/Chat/Enjoy during breaks.
+ Put headphones on ear and work during.
+ Maintain a to-do List.

---
### Question 4
What are the dangers of social media, in brief?
### Answer:
+ Affects our ability to focus.
+ It is addictive.
+ Wastes time.
+ Everybody is putting their best version on social media; comparing that to yourself can cause depression and unhappiness.
